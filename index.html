Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common Microsoft 365 setup questions.

Where can I download Microsoft 365?

Visit Microsoft365.com and sign in with the Microsoft account associated with your purchase or subscription.

How do I activate Office?

Open an Office app and sign in with the account associated with your eligible license or subscription. Follow any activation instructions displayed.

What if I already purchased Office?

Sign in to the Microsoft account used for the purchase and check your services, subscriptions or order history.

Where can I get official Microsoft support?

Visit Microsoft Support for official troubleshooting and available contact options.
